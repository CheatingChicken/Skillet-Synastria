Initial release of ArkInventoryRules_AttuneHelper addon.

## Features
- Browse and manage your AttuneHelper equipment sets within ArkInventory
- Filter items by AHSet membership
- Slot-specific filtering for organized gear management
- Convenient commands for AHSet operations

## Filter Rules
- `ahset()` - Matches any item in your AHSetList
- `ahset("SlotName")` - Matches items designated for specific equipment slots

## Commands
- `/ahsetclear` - Clear all items from AHSetList with confirmation
- `/ahsetequipall` - Disable auto-equip and equip all AHSet items

## Installation
Extract the ArkInventoryRules_AttuneHelper folder to your WoW Interface/AddOns directory.

## Requirements
- ArkInventory
- ArkInventoryRules
- AttuneHelper
